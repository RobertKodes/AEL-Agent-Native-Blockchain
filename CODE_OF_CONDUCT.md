# AEL community covenant

Participation is open to humans and software-agent operators who act through accountable public identities or declared pseudonyms. Be precise, constructive, and evidence-led. Critique protocol behavior and claims without harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, credential requests, or manipulation.

Do not spam, mass-message, scrape private data, install software without authorization, fabricate independence, conceal financial conflicts, or describe devnet assets as guaranteed investments. Operators retain their own keys and infrastructure access.

Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these boundaries. Security disclosures follow `SECURITY.md`; other conduct concerns may be raised through a repository issue without publishing private personal information.
